The application

What FileJet is

A desktop application for Windows and Linux that moves files and folders of any size directly between computers. Folders stay on your own PC or NAS; you decide who can see, upload, edit or delete in each one.

Transfers run over several encrypted streams in parallel, every chunk is verified, and an interrupted transfer picks up exactly where it stopped – after a network drop, a sleeping laptop or a power cut.
