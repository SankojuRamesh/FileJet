Features

Fast. Direct.
Under your control.

Everything FileJet does to move large files and folders between people – quickly, safely and without a cloud in between.

Share

Folders on your own storage

Share any folder on your PC, external drive or NAS with one person or a whole team. Watch deliveries arrive and keep a full record.

  • Share folders and Submit (drop-box) folders
  • Received-files log with sender, time, size and form details
  • “Arriving now” with live size, speed and time left
  • Free-space display – no storage to rent

Permissions

Everyone gets exactly the access they need

Decide per person and per folder. Every request is checked by the folder owner’s app – changes apply instantly, even to running transfers.

  • View & download · Upload · Edit · Delete
  • Roles: Uploader, Viewer, Editor, Manager – or custom
  • Groups, access expiry and invitation e-mails with the folder ID
  • Rules: allowed file types, maximum size, required form fields
{% include "website/_pic.html" with name="shared" alt="Shared folder with permissions" w=1240 h=700 %}
Transfer

Fast, verified and unstoppable

Files are split into chunks and sent over several encrypted streams at once. Every chunk is verified before it counts.

  • Automatic resume after network or power loss
  • Outbox: send now, delivered when the recipient is online
  • Sending and receiving progress with sizes and time left
  • Works in the office and across the internet (UPnP, STUN, hole punching)
{% include "website/_pic.html" with name="transfers" alt="Transfers with live progress" w=1240 h=700 %}
Team

Online status and chat, next to your files

See who is available and talk about the work without switching apps.

  • Online / offline status for everyone you work with
  • End-to-end encrypted messages with delivered and read ticks
  • Messages to offline people are delivered when they come back
{% include "website/_pic.html" with name="chat" alt="Built-in team chat" w=1240 h=700 %}
Users

Your people, organised

Add teammates by their ID, group them, and give a whole group access to a folder in one step.

  • Add by 9-digit ID, username or e-mail
  • Groups such as Editors, Colorists or Clients
  • One click to chat with anyone
