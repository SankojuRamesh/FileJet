Features

Fast. Direct.
Under your control.

Everything FileJet does to move large files and folders between people – quickly, safely and without a cloud in between.

Workspaces

Workspaces on your own storage

Turn any folder on your PC, external drive or NAS into a workspace. Organise it as a folder tree, upload files or whole folders, and see every file of the workspace in one list.

  • Workspace cards with size, people and free drive space at a glance
  • Folder tree: create folders inside folders, rename, delete, upload in one click
  • Share workspaces and Submit (drop-box) workspaces
  • Received-files log and “arriving now” with live speed and time left
{% include "website/_pic.html" with name="folders" alt="Workspaces with folder tree" w=1240 h=700 %}
Sharing

Share the whole workspace – or just one folder

Pick a folder in the tree and share only that folder: it stays where it is, nothing is copied, and the people you choose see that folder alone. Pick your users or groups from one list.

  • Share one folder of a workspace without creating anything new
  • Pick people and groups from a drop-down – add new people by their ID
  • Stop sharing any time – the folder and its files stay
  • Invitation e-mails, one-time codes and upload approval
{% include "website/_pic.html" with name="share" alt="Share only one folder with your users or groups" w=1240 h=700 %}
Permissions

Everyone gets exactly the access they need

Decide per person and per folder. Every request is checked by the owner’s app – changes apply instantly, even to running transfers.

  • View & download · Upload · Edit · Delete – download only when you allow it
  • Roles: Uploader, Viewer, Contributor (view, download & upload), Editor, Manager – or custom
  • Groups get exactly the permissions you tick, with access expiry
  • Rules: allowed file types, maximum size, required form fields
{% include "website/_pic.html" with name="shared" alt="A shared folder seen by a team member" w=1240 h=700 %}
Transfer

Fast, verified and unstoppable

Files are split into chunks and sent over several encrypted streams at once. Every chunk is verified before it counts.

  • Automatic resume after network or power loss
  • Outbox: send now, delivered when the recipient is online
  • Sending and receiving progress with sizes and time left
  • Works in the office and across the internet (UPnP, STUN, hole punching)
{% include "website/_pic.html" with name="transfers" alt="Transfers with live progress" w=1240 h=700 %}
Team

Online status and chat, next to your files

See who is available and talk about the work without switching apps.

  • Online / offline status for everyone you work with
  • End-to-end encrypted messages with delivered and read ticks
  • Messages to offline people are delivered when they come back
{% include "website/_pic.html" with name="chat" alt="Built-in team chat" w=1240 h=700 %}
Users

Your people, organised

Add teammates by their ID, group them, and give a whole group access to a folder in one step.

  • Add by 9-digit ID, username or e-mail
  • Groups such as Editors, Colorists or Clients
  • One click to chat with anyone
