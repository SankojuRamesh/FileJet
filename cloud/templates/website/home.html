FAQ

Questions, answered

Where are my files stored?

Only on your own computers and storage. FileJet sends files directly between the two computers; our servers handle sign-in, permissions and transfer status only.

Is there a file size limit?

No limit on Pro and Business. The Free plan allows files up to 20 GB.

What if the connection drops during a transfer?

FileJet resumes automatically from the last verified chunk when the connection is back – also after a restart.

Can I send to someone who is offline?

Yes. Files wait in your outbox and are delivered automatically as soon as the folder owner comes online.

Which systems are supported?

Windows 10/11 and Ubuntu/Debian Linux (64-bit). The web dashboard works in any modern browser.
